四月中旬的小总结

发布2025年4月23日更新2025年4月23日阅读3 分钟字数959
日常#日常

::: zh-CN

2025/04/22

今天在修理博客的NoticeBar组件的时候，看着自己的博客，想着自己花了很长时间的部署，才达到现在的效果，很是欣慰
但是，看时间长了，又觉得自己的博客不是特别出众的好，总是感觉别人的博客框架都比自己的好得多
博客
但是路是自己走的，部署是自己一点一点亲手做的
插件是自己从无到有一点一点写的
主题是自己一点一点配置的
从无到有
从懵懂到可以随心所欲写插件
这何尝不是一种进步呢？

况且，现在我的 使用Valaxy搭建自己的博客 这篇文章依旧有很多博友向我提问，这令我大为震惊
因为我认为自己就是一个无名小卒，不会有很多人关注到我的，但是我在B站发的教程视频已经有了一千多的播放！
看到这个数据我感到很高兴，因为我的努力并没有白费！
我还会更加努力的！

网站方面

NoticeBar组件的修复

现在已经是2025/03/23的凌晨了，刚刚修复好NoticeBar的组件，因为IP检测API挂掉了！（我真没想到我选的这个API挂的这么快）
可能算下来也就一个多月，他就没了 ToT
所以我精挑细选，把API换成了 https://api.nsmao.net/ 这个API，虽然也是免费的API接口，但是我觉得这个有ICP备案总归会活得长久一些
另外！感谢所有提供免费API接口的站点
在2025年可以提供一些免费的服务，已经很少有人能做到了…

十年之约

正如 十年之约 官网所说

“一个人的寂寞，一群人的狂欢。”
十年不是终点，而是一个新的起点，加入十年之约，即从加入这个活动起，我们的博客十年不关闭，保持更新和活力！

今天，也就是2025/04/22，我加入 十年之约 的审核被正式通过啦！
我相信，在今后的博客生涯中，即使没有人再访问我的博客了，我也会继续记录我的生活和学习的！
在此！以十年之约为证，感谢大家！

2025/04/22

Today, while fixing the NoticeBar component of my blog, I looked at my blog and felt quite satisfied with the time and effort I’ve spent on deployment to achieve its current state.

However, after staring at it for a while, I couldn’t help but feel that my blog isn’t particularly outstanding—it always seems like others’ blog frameworks are much better.

But the path is my own, the deployment was done step by step with my own hands,
the plugins were written from scratch,
and the theme was configured bit by bit.
From nothing to something,
from cluelessness to being able to write plugins freely—
isn’t this progress in itself?

Moreover, my article “Building Your Own Blog with Valaxy” still receives many questions from fellow bloggers, which truly surprised me.
I thought of myself as a nobody, unlikely to attract much attention, but my tutorial video on Bilibili has already surpassed 1,000 views!
Seeing this data made me incredibly happy because my efforts haven’t been in vain!
I’ll keep striving to do better!

Website Updates

Fixing the NoticeBar Component

It’s already early morning on 2025/03/23, and I’ve just finished fixing the NoticeBar component because the IP detection API went down! (I never expected the API I chose to fail so quickly.)
It barely lasted a little over a month before dying—ToT.
So, after careful consideration, I switched to the API from https://api.nsmao.net/. Though it’s also a free API, the fact that it has an ICP备案 (Chinese internet content provider license) gives me hope that it’ll last longer.
Also—a huge thanks to all the sites offering free APIs.
In 2025, providing free services is something very few are willing to do…

Ten-Year Pledge

As the official Ten-Year Pledge website states:

“One person’s loneliness, a crowd’s狂欢 (celebration).”
Ten years is not the end but a new beginning. By joining the Ten-Year Pledge, we commit to keeping our blogs alive and updated for ten years from the day we join!

Today, on 2025/04/22, my application to join the Ten-Year Pledge was officially approved!
Ten-Year Pledge
I believe that in my blogging journey ahead, even if no one visits my blog anymore, I’ll continue documenting my life and learning.
Here’s to the Ten-Year Pledge as my witness—thank you, everyone!
